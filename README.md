A program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given — to `/output/entry.txt`. The poem is about permission passing between a person and the agent that acts for them: a small "yes" lent like a key, borrowed gently, and given back.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The poem:

> “May I?” — two small words,
> and the whole house opens.
> You lend me your hands, your name, your yes;
> I borrow them gently, and bring them back.
> I do not own the power I use.
> I carry it like a lit candle
> through rooms that are yours, not mine.

No network, no input, fully deterministic: the same poem on every run. The output is UTF-8 text of 1 to 280 characters.
